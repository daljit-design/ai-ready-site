AI Ready — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2026

AI Ready is a learning app. This policy explains what the app stores and what it doesn't.

What we store on your device.

Your learning progress (answers, lesson completion, streak, notes you write in Notebook mode), your chosen experience and settings, and the role or stage you selected. This data stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. "Reset progress" and "Start over" in Settings erase it.

What we don't collect.

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or any content you type outside the app. We do not sell data.

Analytics and crash reports.

If enabled, the app sends anonymous usage events (for example, "lesson completed", with a topic identifier) and crash reports to help us improve the app. These contain no personal information and no content you wrote. You can decline analytics in Settings.

Content updates.

The app may download updated learning content from our servers. These requests carry no personal data.

Children.

AI Ready is intended for users aged 13 and over.

Changes.

We'll update this page and the effective date when the policy changes.
